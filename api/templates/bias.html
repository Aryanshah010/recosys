{% call ui.method("What these metrics mean") %}

Filter Bubble Score is the fraction of the top 10 matching both the user's preferred language and at least one preferred genre. Preference Escape@10 is its exact complement — deliberately not called "novelty", which in the literature means self-information over item popularity, not this. Read from results/rq2_*_{{ track }}.csv.

The Affinity_Only ablation reaches a bubble score of 1.000 and an escape of 0.000 by construction: it scores exactly the predicate the metric measures. It is a reference point, not a measurement. The hybrid's job is to buy accuracy back without going there.

{% call ui.method("Why λ = 0.20 rather than the optimum") %}
