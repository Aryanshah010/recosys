Significance on NDCG@10

{% call ui.method("How significance was tested") %}

Every model pair tested with a paired t-test and a Wilcoxon signed-rank test, since NDCG is heavily zero-inflated. Holm–Bonferroni corrected across the 15-comparison family, with paired effect sizes (Cohens_dz) and explicit win/tie/loss counts, because a mean improvement can be driven by a minority of users while most are unchanged. Rows surviving correction at α = 0.05 are highlighted.
